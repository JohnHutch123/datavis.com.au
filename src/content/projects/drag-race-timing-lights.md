## Overview

Drag Race Timing Lights (shown on phones as **Drag Tree**) simulates a drag racing starting tree so you can
practise your launches anywhere. It is a Delphi 12 FireMonkey app for iPhone and Android, and also runs on
Windows.

## Features

- **Pro or Sportsman tree:** Pro lights all three ambers together with the green 0.400 s later; Sportsman lights them one after another, 0.500 s apart.
- **Realistic staging:** pre-stage and stage beams, deep staging, and a "too far forward" fault.
- **Pedals:** brake, plus optional manual clutch and throttle pads you use with several fingers at once. Stall the engine or bog the launch just like a real car.
- **Reaction times** measured from the moment the green is due, with red-light, perfect, good and late results.
- **History and stats** of your runs, saved on your device.
- **Private:** no account, no ads, no tracking, works offline.

## Reaction times

| Time | Meaning |
|---|---|
| below 0.000 | Red light (you left too soon) |
| 0.000–0.009 | Perfect light |
| 0.010–0.099 | Great / good light |
| 0.100 and up | Late |

## Overview

Drill and Blast is a new generation of Datavis software for designing and managing blasts in mines and
quarries, building on many years of earlier Datavis drill and blast tools.

## Planned features

- **Blast design:** lay out patterns, holes and charging, with a shared engineering maths library.
- **Timing analysis:** design and check initiation timing.
- **Field data:** exchange timing data with blasting equipment and loggers, and import drilled and blasted data back.
- **GIS output:** export designs as shapefiles from templates, for mine planning and survey systems.
- **Editions:** **Standard** for everyday design and **Professional** for advanced analysis.

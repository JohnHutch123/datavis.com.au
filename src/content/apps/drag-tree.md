## About the app

Drag Tree simulates a drag racing starting tree so you can practise your launches anywhere.

- **Pro or Sportsman tree** with realistic amber and green timing.
- **Staging** with pre-stage and stage beams, deep staging and a too-far-forward fault.
- **Pedals:** brake, plus optional clutch and throttle. Stall or bog the car just like the real thing.
- **Reaction times** with red-light, perfect, good and late results, and a history of your runs.
- **Private:** no account, no ads, no tracking, works offline.

See [How to use Drag Tree](/docs/drag-race-timing-lights/how-to-use/) for a full guide.

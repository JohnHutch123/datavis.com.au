## Settings

Tap **Settings** to choose:

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **Tree** | **Pro**: all three ambers light together, green 0.400 s later. **Sportsman** (full tree): ambers light one after another 0.500 s apart, green 0.500 s after the last. |
| **Lane** | Left or Right. Your lane is marked **YOU** in blue, and your car is the blue one. |
| **Staging** | **Auto** rolls your car into stage. **Manual**: ease off the brake to creep forward, press it to stop. |
| **Transmission** | **Manual** adds a **CLUTCH** pedal. Stop on the brake with the clutch out and the engine stalls. |
| **Throttle** | **Manual** adds a **THROTTLE** pedal you hold for full revs. The pad fills up like a rev meter. |

Your settings are saved between sessions.

## The pedals

Pedals are pads at the bottom of the screen: clutch on the left, brake in the middle, throttle on the
right. Use several fingers at once like real pedals; you never need more than two at a time. On Windows,
without a touch screen, only **BRAKE** is used and the space bar works as the brake.

## A run

1. **Press Start.** Both cars roll up to the beams. The pre-stage bulb lights when your front tyre breaks the pre-stage beam, and the stage bulb 7 inches later. Roll past the pre-stage window and you are deep staged. Roll right through the stage beam and you get **TOO FAR FORWARD**, which ends the run.
2. **Hold the car.**
   - Automatic: hold **BRAKE** (and **THROTTLE** too with manual throttle).
   - Manual: stop with **BRAKE** and **CLUTCH** together. Once staged, keep the clutch in and move your right finger from the brake to the **THROTTLE**.
3. **Wait for the tree.** Once both lanes are staged, the tree starts after a random 0.8 to 3.0 s.
4. **Launch** by letting the car go: release the brake (automatic) or drop the clutch (manual). Launching with the throttle closed bogs the car; dropping the clutch with the brake on, or with no throttle, stalls it.

## Reaction times

Your reaction time is measured from when the green is due to the moment you let the car go.

| Time | Meaning |
|---|---|
| below 0.000 | Red light: you left too soon |
| 0.000–0.009 | Perfect light |
| 0.010–0.099 | Great / good light |
| 0.100 and up | Late |

If you red-light, your red bulb lights and the tree carries on for the other lane. History marks
Sportsman runs with **S**, red lights with **R** and bogged launches with **B**.

## Buttons

**Start** runs the tree (or press **Enter** on Windows), **Stop** aborts a run, **Reset** clears the lights
for the next run, and **Clear** wipes the history and stats.

## Timing accuracy

Times come from a high-resolution stopwatch, and your tap is timestamped as soon as it touches the screen.
Phone screens and touch panels add some delay, typically 20 to 60 ms combined, which the app does not
subtract.

## Who it is for

Drill and blast engineers and supervisors in open-cut mines and quarries who design blasts, manage
drilling and charging, and need their designs to flow to field equipment and mine planning systems.

## Workflow

1. **Design** the blast pattern: holes, burden and spacing, depths and charging.
2. **Time** the blast and analyse the initiation sequence.
3. **Export** timing data to blasting equipment and loggers, and the design to GIS as shapefiles.
4. **Review** what was drilled and blasted against the design.

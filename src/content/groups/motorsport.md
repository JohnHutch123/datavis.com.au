## The projects

**Drag Race Timing Lights** is a phone app that simulates a drag racing starting tree (Pro or Sportsman)
so you can practise reaction times, with realistic staging, clutch, brake and throttle pedals.

**RaceBox Data** reads a RaceBox Mini, Mini S or Micro over Bluetooth LE from a Windows PC: live GPS and
motion data at 25 Hz and the sessions recorded on the device. It is the data-capture step for lap timing,
track maps and run reviews.

Both are written in Delphi.
